About Vertex Metals

A trade-focused intermediary, built for UK industry's global sourcing needs

Vertex Metals was established to source industrial metals and strategic minerals from global manufacturers and supply to UK industrial buyers — with quality, trust, and sustainability at the core of everything we do.

Company

What we do

Vertex Metals Ltd is a steel and metals trading intermediary, positioned between international mills and UK-based steel service centres and industrial buyers. We act as principal in every trade — sourcing materials directly from vetted global suppliers and delivering to UK buyers under well-documented, internationally compliant contracts.

UK metals procurement has traditionally been shaped by long-standing relationships with established mills. Vertex Metals introduces a broader, more dynamic sourcing model — giving buyers access to a wider portfolio of qualified international suppliers evaluated against their specific requirements. Our supplier auditing function bridges the trust gap that typically limits exploration of competitive global alternatives, delivering greater choice, improved pricing transparency, and the compliance standards UK procurement teams require.

Trusted Intermediary

We are not a broker. We purchase from the supplier and sell to the buyer — acting as the contractual counterparty in every transaction. One compliant entity for your procurement, legal, and finance teams to deal with.

Audited Supplier Network

Every mill in our network is evaluated against our AML/KYC, sanctions, and technical quality criteria before any trade is executed. This includes assessment of metallurgical process controls and compliance with recognised British and European product specifications. Pre-shipment samples can be arranged to verify material quality against your specification before committing to volume.

Full Trade Documentation

Every shipment is documented to the standard UK procurement teams require — bills of lading, certificates of origin, mill test reports, and CBAM carbon-intensity data where applicable.

Trading Model

How our intermediary model works

We act as principal in every trade. UK buyers have a single, compliant counterparty to deal with. International suppliers gain access to a reliable, qualified UK buyer.

01

Source & Qualify

We identify and qualify suppliers globally against our AML/KYC, sanctions, and technical quality standards — including metallurgical process controls, product specification compliance, and the capacity to produce verifiable mill test reports against British and European standards. Only mills that meet our criteria enter the network.

02

Price RFQs

UK buyers submit requests for quotation. We obtain competitive quotes from our global supplier network and provide landed UK pricing inclusive of applicable duties and freight.

03

Execute Trade

We act as principal — purchasing from the international supplier and selling to the UK buyer. We manage Letters of Credit, Bills of Lading, certificates of origin, and all trade documentation.

04

Logistics & Delivery

We coordinate sea freight, customs clearance, and delivery to the UK buyer's specified destination, maintaining oversight at every stage of transit.

Quality & Standards

Built to ISO 9001 principles from day one

Vertex Metals' systems, processes, and trade documentation are designed to comply with ISO 9001 quality management principles from the outset of operations. We are not retrofitting compliance after the fact — we are building the way we want to be audited from the start.

ISO 9001 Accreditation — targeted after year one of operations

Our trade documentation workflows, supplier qualification processes, order management procedures, and internal review cycles are all structured to ISO 9001 requirements. We are actively pursuing formal accreditation following our first full year of trading. The accreditation will validate a way of working that is already embedded in how we operate.

Mill Quality Standards

Our supplier qualification process assesses metallurgical process controls — smelting procedures, cooling protocols, and finishing treatments — against relevant British and European specifications including BS EN 10025, EN 10088, and ASTM equivalents. Quality is assessed at source, not on arrival.

Pre-Shipment Sampling

For new materials, new supplier relationships, or tight-tolerance specifications, we can arrange independent pre-shipment sampling and testing. Buyers receive confirmation that product meets their specification before full-volume shipment is authorised.

Full Regulatory Framework

AML/KYC, sanctions screening, CBAM, EORI, VAT, and Isle of Man Companies Act compliance are all embedded into our operating structure — not managed reactively when an issue arises.

Trade Documentation Standard

Every trade is documented to the standard expected by UK procurement, legal, and finance teams: bills of lading, certificates of origin, mill test reports, and CBAM carbon-intensity data where applicable.
