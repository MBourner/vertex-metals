Stage 1a — Supplier Registration

Capture the supplier's identity, company details, address and primary contact. Once submitted, the request goes to the compliance director for sanctions screening and a preliminary risk assessment (Stage 1b) before the supplier becomes "Ready to Quote".

    Company Details

    Manufacturing and Materials / Commodities suppliers follow the full AML/CFT due-diligence track (sanctions re-screening and risk assessment review in Stage 2). Logistics, Packaging and Service Provider suppliers follow the simplified track.
