Overview

Vertex Metals provides access to a comprehensive range of stainless steel grades at highly competitive prices, sourced from top-tier mills across India and the Asia-Pacific. Our network enables direct container shipment to the UK buyer's destination — removing unnecessary handling and compressing lead times.

Stainless steel is a critical material across food processing, pharmaceutical manufacturing, construction, oil & gas, and industrial engineering. We source to specification and supply with full mill certification, ensuring the right grade and form for every application.

All stainless steel products are supplied with mill test reports and certificates of origin as standard.
